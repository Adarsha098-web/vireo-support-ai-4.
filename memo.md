\# Vireo Support AI — Decision Memo



\*\*To:\*\* Priya Raman  

\*\*Subject:\*\* Support routing analysis and AI-assisted categorization  

\*\*Analysis period:\*\* January 2025 to June 2026



\## Executive Summary



The analysis covers 11,641 support tickets from January 2025 through June 2026. Billing is the second-largest first-assigned team by ticket volume, but the data indicates that queue size alone should not be used as the basis for adding headcount.



For the current helpdesk period beginning September 14, 2025, 1,558 tickets were initially assigned to Billing. Of these, 482 were ultimately resolved by a different team, an apparent misrouting rate of 30.9%. These Billing tickets recorded 615 internal transfers, representing approximately ₹187,575 in transfer cost at the policy rate of ₹305 per transfer.



\## Recommendation



Improve categorization and routing before making a headcount decision. The proposed AI-assisted categorizer uses ticket text to predict a support category and suggest a destination team. This can be used as a routing aid at intake, with human review for low-confidence cases.



As a modeled improvement scenario, reducing apparent Billing misrouting from 30.9% to 15% would reduce approximately 248 misrouted tickets at the observed Billing volume. Based on the historical transfer rate, this represents an estimated transfer-cost opportunity of approximately ₹96,628 over the analyzed period.



This is a modeled opportunity rather than guaranteed savings.



\## What the Tool Shows



The prototype provides:



\- Monthly ticket volume by category

\- Monthly ticket volume by team

\- Category and team distribution

\- Billing routing and transfer analysis

\- AI-assisted ticket categorization

\- Suggested destination team

\- Confidence score

\- Human validation workflow



The model's current holdout score measures agreement with the existing intake categories. Because the existing categories may contain routing and labeling issues, this score should not be treated as independently verified accuracy.



\## Hiring Implication



The analysis supports addressing routing quality and process leakage before using raw queue volume as the sole basis for two additional hires. After the routing intervention is measured, Vireo can reassess whether persistent workload requires additional capacity.



\## Next Steps



1\. Pilot AI-assisted categorization on a controlled set of incoming tickets.

2\. Route high-confidence tickets automatically and send low-confidence cases for human review.

3\. Track misrouting, transfers, first-response performance and CSAT.

4\. Reassess Billing and Logistics workload after the pilot.

5\. Use the measured workload after process improvement to inform any future headcount decision.
